## Let's Play!


Gangster of IDD



- 1 Criminal - Recruit one player (except the polices) to be part of the criminal gang at night \pa

- 2 Polices - Know each other and immune to criminal recruit \pa

- 1 Detective - Check the status of a player at night \pa

- 1 Good citizen - Know the detective \pa

- Citizen (the rest) - Help the polices and the detective find criminals. \pa





## Game Flow



- Day - Everyone openly discusses who to catch. \pa If successful, the caught criminal is out of the game, and you may attempt to catch again. \pa If failed, the caught player is still in play.\pa

- Night - Each criminal, individually, recruits one new team member.\pa

Win condition: \pa


- Criminal team wins if all but the polices are part of the criminal gang. \pa

- Police team (polices, detective, and citizens) wins if they catch all the criminals.
