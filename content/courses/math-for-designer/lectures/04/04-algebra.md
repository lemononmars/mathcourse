## Algebra of Functions

Algebra of functions is the operation of the functions, specifically the arithematic operations.

:::property

Let $f$ and $g$ be functions and $c$ be a constant.


- $(cf) (x) = c ( f(x) )$ 

- $(f+g) (x) = f(x) + g(x)$ 

- $(f-g) (x) = f(x) - g(x)$

- $(f \cdot g) (x) = f(x) \cdot g(x) $

- $ \left(\frac{f}{g}\right)(x) = \frac{f(x)}{g(x)}$ where $g(x) \neq 0$


:::






:::example

Let $f(x) = x+1$ and $g(x) = 2x-3$. Find the followings.


- $(2f)(x)$

- $(f+g)(x)$ 

- $(f - g)(x)$

- $(3f + 2g)(x)$


:::


:::solution

\begin{eqnarray*}
(2f)(x) &=& 2f(x) 

&=& 2(x+1) = 2x+2 

(f+g)(x) &=& (x+1) + (2x-3) 

&=& (x+2x) + (1-3) = 3x-2 

(f-g)(x) &=& (x+1) - (2x-3) 

&=& (x+1) + (-2x + 3) = (x-2x) + (1+3) = -x+4 

(3f + 2g) &=& 3f(x) + 2g(x) 

&=& 3(x+1) + 2(2x-3) = (3x+3) + (4x-6) = (7x-3)
\end{eqnarray*}

:::






:::example

Let $f(x) = x+1$ and $g(x) = 2x-3$. Find the followings.


- $(fg)(x)$

- $\left( \frac{f}{g} \right)(x)$


:::


:::solution

\begin{eqnarray*}
(fg)(x) &=& (x+1)(2x-3) 

&=& 2x^2-3x+2x-3 = 2x^2-x-3

\left(\frac{f}{g} \right)(x)&=& \frac{x+1}{2x-3}
\end{eqnarray*}
We can leave the fraction as is.

:::
