## A Closer Look

Notice the formula
\[ \bar{X} \sim N\left( \mu, \frac{\sigma_x}{\sqrt{n}}\right).\]
This implies that as $n$ gets larger and larger, the variance 
\[\sigma_{\bar{X}} = \frac{\sigma_x}{\sqrt{n}}\]
gets smaller and smaller. Recall that small variance implies narrow spread, and thus $\bar{X}$ would get closer to the mena $\mu_x$.




## Law of Large Numbers


:::theorem

Let $X$ be a random variable with mean $\mu$. If you take samples of size $n$ where $n$ is very large, then the mean $\bar{X}$ of the sample tends to get closer and closer to $\mu$.

:::

In other words, your basic intuition works!
