## What is Descriptive Statistics?


:::note The Goal

To **organize**, **summarize**, and **simplify** data so it is easier to understand.

:::





\column{.45\textwidth}
**We want to...**


- Find patterns

- Visualize relationships

- Compact information

\column{.45\textwidth}

:::figure facebook_analytic
:::






## Two Main Branches


\column{.48\textwidth}

:::note 1. Descriptive

Summarizing data from a **sample**.


- Charts/Graphs

- Averages (Mean, Median)

- Spread (Std. Deviation)


:::

\column{.48\textwidth}


:::note 2. Inferential

Drawing conclusions about a **population** based on a sample.


- Hypothesis Testing

- Prediction/Estimation

- P-values


:::






\fbox{Descriptive $\longrightarrow$ Information} \quad \fbox{Inferential $\longrightarrow$ Decisions}




## Types of Data

Before we visualize, we must know the type of data:


- Labels or names.


- e.g., Gender, Hair Color, Blood Type.


- Numbers representing counts or measurements.


- **Discrete**: Countable (e.g., Number of pets).

- **Continuous**: Measurable (e.g., Height, Weight).

## Chart Selection Guide




| **Chart Type** | **Data Type** | **Best usage** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bar Chart | Qualitative | Comparing categories |
| Pie Chart | Qualitative | Parts of a whole (\%) |
| Histogram | Quantitative | Distribution/Ranges |
| Line Graph | Quantitative | Trends over time |

## Histograms (Quantitative)


\column{.55\textwidth}
In a **histogram**, the horizontal axis is **numerical** and **continuous**.


- Bars touch each other.

- Shows the **shape** of data.

\column{.4\textwidth}

:::figure covid_age_death_rate — Age Distribution
:::






## Misleading Graphs


\column{.55\textwidth}
**Watch out for:**


- **Y-axis manipulation**: Starting from non-zero.

- **3D Effects**: Distorts proportions.

- **Missing labels**: What are the units?
