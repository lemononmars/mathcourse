## Definition of Statistics


:::note Then: ``State-tistics''

Collection of population and economic information vital to the **state**.

:::






:::note Now: Statistics

A scientific method of **collection**, **analysis**, and **interpretation** of data, widely applied in business and all the social and natural sciences.

:::





## Key Terms



- Collection of observations (measurements, genders, survey responses). 

- The complete collection of **all** individuals to be studied. 

- A **sub-collection** of members selected from a population.





## Parameter vs. Statistic



- \textbf{**P**arameter}: a numerical measurement describing some characteristic of a \textbf{**P**opulation}. 

- \textbf{**S**tatistic}: a numerical measurement describing some characteristic of a \textbf{**S**ample}.





\fbox{ **P**opulation $\longrightarrow$ **P**arameter} 


\fbox{ **S**ample $\longrightarrow$ **S**tatistic}

## Probability: The Study of Uncertainty



- **Probability** is the mathematical tool used to study randomness.

- It deals with the **chance** of an event occurring.

- It forms the foundation for **Inferential Statistics**.





*``Statistics is the science of learning from data, and probability is the study of uncertainty.''*
